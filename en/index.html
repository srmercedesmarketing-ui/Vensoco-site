Privacy & data

The forms on this page collect your name, your project or skill information, and your email, solely so the Vensoco team can reach out about your signup. We don't sell or share this data with third parties, and we don't use it for anything other than responding to you. Submissions are processed through Google Forms before reaching our inbox.

If you'd like us to delete your information, email us at contacto@vensoco.com. As explained above, Vensoco never takes part in negotiations or transactions between users — this notice covers only the contact details you give us directly when you sign up.
